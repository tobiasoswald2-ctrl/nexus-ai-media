Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026.

Nexus AI Media is a development project for media analytics and workflow automation. This policy explains how data is handled when a user authorizes a connected platform such as TikTok.

Data we may process

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, Nexus AI Media may process basic authorized account information and public video performance data such as video identifiers, titles, duration, view counts, like counts, comment counts, share counts, and share URLs.

How data is used

Data is used to measure content performance, compare publishing and editing decisions, improve internal media workflows, and support performance analytics.

Authorization

Nexus AI Media accesses platform data only when the relevant account holder explicitly authorizes the requested permissions. Users may revoke platform authorization through the relevant third-party platform where supported.

Data sharing

Nexus AI Media does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure and platform providers as necessary to operate the service.

Data retention

Authorized analytics data may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for workflow analysis, debugging, performance comparison, and legal or security purposes, unless deletion is required earlier.

Security

Access credentials and tokens are intended to be stored securely and separately from public website content. Sensitive credentials are never published in this repository.

Third-party services

Connected services such as TikTok process data under their own privacy policies and terms.

Questions or deletion requests

For questions about this development project or requests concerning stored data, contact the project owner through the public GitHub repository associated with Nexus AI Media.
